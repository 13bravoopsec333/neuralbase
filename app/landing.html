    FAQ

    Is Neuralbase a game?

    It borrows the shape of one. Each drill has a score, a timer, and a level that moves with your accuracy.

    Will it raise my IQ?

    Neuralbase makes no claim about IQ, and we cannot promise you one. What the research supports is that training carries over to closely related untrained tasks, which is what these drills target. Whether that reaches general intelligence is an open question the current evidence does not settle.

    How long is a session?

    A set takes about three minutes. Run a single drill if you have less time, or build a longer circuit on Pro.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, from settings at any time. Your account returns to the free plan and your history stays.

    Is my data saved?

    Yes. Your scores, settings, and plan save to your account and sync across devices. In demo mode, everything stays in this browser.

    What does it cost?

    Free is $0. Pro is $4.99 per month. The plans above list what each one includes.
