Concept C

Plain essay

The whole position as plain prose, read top to bottom with no tables.

MethodConcept C, Plain essay

What each drill does, and what the research found

Each of the nine drills trains one specific skill, and training moves your score on that skill and on tasks much like it. How far that carries is the harder question, and the rest of this page answers it.

Neuralbase runs nine short drills drawn from cognitive-training research: working memory, processing speed, memory strategy, reasoning, recall, mental flexibility, sustained attention, reaction speed, and mental arithmetic.

The evidence is not uniform across them. Speed of Processing shows the strongest durability found, held at 10 years in the ACTIVE trial. Relational Reasoning is the strongest transfer lever found, d=0.50 across 57 experiments. Memory Palace transfers to untrained memory material, durable 4 to 32 months.

Executive N-Back shows near transfer to working memory is reliable. Spaced Retrieval is retrieval practice plus spacing, the highest real-world yield. Task Switching shows executive flexibility with near transfer.

Signal Alert improves trained vigilance and near transfer to similar watching tasks, not to general attention or grades. Simple Reaction shrinks the specific basic speed-accuracy trade-off, and reliable broad speedup elsewhere is not shown. Mental Arithmetic lifts the trained skill and shows some near transfer to numeracy, with no IQ change.

Each drill trains its own skill, and near transfer to similar tasks is reliable. Broad transfer to general intelligence is not demonstrated, so Neuralbase does not claim it.

Grades, test scores, jobs, and income sit outside what any of this evidence measures. Neuralbase has run no trials of its own, so none of these figures are ours to defend.

Train one drill, run the daily circuit, or build your own. Interleaving drills and spacing your reviews are the design choices with the most support.
